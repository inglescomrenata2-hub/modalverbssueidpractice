Key language

can  ·  can't  ·  have to  ·  don't have to  ·  should  ·  shouldn't

can → it is OK / it is allowed. You can use the meeting room.

can't → it is not allowed. You can't share student information.

have to → it is necessary (a rule). We have to sign the enrollment form.

don't have to → it is not necessary, but it is OK. You don't have to come on Friday.

should → good advice. You should answer emails quickly.

shouldn't → bad idea. You shouldn't be late for meetings.

⚠️ can't and don't have to are different! Can't = not allowed. Don't have to = not necessary.

Part A — Situations at work

Read each situation. Write ONE sentence with the modal verb in the box.

  1. Every new student needs to sign an enrollment form. This is a rule. have to
  2. Students can pay online or at the office. They can choose. don't have to
  3. It is not allowed to share a student's phone number with other people. can't
  4. The meeting room is free this afternoon. You are allowed to use it. can
  5. Your colleague has 40 new emails and feels stressed. Give advice. should
  6. A colleague is always late to meetings. Give advice. shouldn't
  7. Your boss says the Friday meeting is optional. don't have to

  9. A student forgot an important document for enrollment. Give advice. should

Many answers are possible. Examples:

1. New students have to sign an enrollment form.

2. Students don't have to pay at the office.

3. We can't share a student's phone number.

4. You can use the meeting room this afternoon.

5. You should answer the urgent emails first.

6. You shouldn't be late for meetings.

7. We don't have to go to the meeting on Friday.

8. I can help you with the prices.

9. The student should send the document by email today.

10. We have to close the front door.
